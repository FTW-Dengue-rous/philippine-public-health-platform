# Business question register

Owner: LUNASaan project team
Last updated: 2026-10-21
Status: intended scope; no BQ1 result is published from `main`.

## BQ1 — active question

Which areas show the largest gaps in **recorded** health-service availability relative to population and selected indicators of disease burden, and how do these gaps differ by socioeconomic conditions?

### Intended decisions

- Prioritise areas for review of facilities, beds, primary care, and workforce availability.
- Identify where local validation is needed before resource allocation.
- Compare potential gaps alongside poverty and selected disease indicators.

### Interpretation safeguards

- Do not equate a listed facility with capacity or quality of care.
- Do not interpret no registry record as proof that no facility exists.
- Do not compare incompatible years, denominators, definitions, or geographic levels without an explicit rule.
- Keep missing values distinct from zero.
- Distance measures require a stated origin, method, and limits; none is implemented on `main`.

Supporting questions may decompose BQ1, but do not become separate business questions unless they have an owner, decision, source, metric definition, and evidence.
