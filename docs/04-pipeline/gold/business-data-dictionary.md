# Gold business data dictionary

Owner: LUNASaan Documentation Team
Last updated: 2026-10-21

| Term | Meaning for this platform |
|---|---|
| Recorded facility | A facility listed in the relevant registry or source at its stated as-of date. Not listed does not mean absent. |
| Planning unit | Province excluding its HUCs, an HUC, or NCR; the analysis unit used where the coverage map permits comparison. |
| Coverage basis | The geography a source's value represents, such as a municipality, HUC-excluded province, region, or national total. |
| Value status | Whether a value is usable, suppressed, missing, or excluded. A null is not zero. |
| Preferred source | The reviewed first-choice source for a metric, area level, and period in `source_priority`. |
| Comparison eligible | A flag showing the source coverage and denominator permit the intended comparison. |
| Authorized beds | Licensed beds, not staffed beds or actual available beds. |
| Disease burden | A reported or modelled measure from its named source. It does not establish causality. |

Formulas, denominator choices, and null rules are in [metric definitions](metric-definitions.md). The full technical column dictionary is [gold-data-dictionary.md](gold-data-dictionary.md).
