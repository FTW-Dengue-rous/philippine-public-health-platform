# Quality

Owner: LUNASaan Documentation Team
Last updated: 2026-10-21

Quality checks prevent invalid or stale bronze, silver, and gold outputs from progressing through the pipeline. The authoritative pages are the [quality-control framework](quality-control-framework.md), [check catalog](quality-check-catalog.md), [lineage and quality contract](lineage-and-quality-contract.md), and [recorded results](quality-results.md).

Use the framework to understand gates and severity, the catalog to identify a check code, the contract to trace a published result, and the results page with [`06-evidence/`](../../06-evidence/README.md) to evaluate a recorded run.
