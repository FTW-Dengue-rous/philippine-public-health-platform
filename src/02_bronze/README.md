# Bronze Layer

Raw data ingested as-is, plus ingestion metadata (source, load timestamp).

## Confirmed source grouping

Only datasets with a provider confirmed in `docs/data_dictionary.md` are grouped below.

- `facility_registry/doh/` — hospital list and infirmary
- `facility_registry/nhfr/` — active facilities
- `facility_registry/psa/` — primary care facilities
- `health_workforce/psa/` — government medical practitioners

Services and capacity datasets remain ungrouped until their source providers are documented.
